Add missed cases

Upload a person crop, or a short clip (up to 20 seconds / 30 MB). Clips contribute up to eight 1-second snapshots. For full scenes, draw a box around one person on each snapshot before labeling. To label a second person in the same frame, upload the image again. Include non-use too.

No person and Unclear are excluded from binary phone-use training. Labeling alone does not change the running detector. Training needs at least 20 use and 20 no-use images, with separate camera-day groups containing both classes. Stop live cameras before training or switching models.

Train & validate

Train an ImageNet-pretrained MobileNetV2 classification head on reviewed person crops or saved person boxes. Validation holds out entire camera-days and compares against the current base model at the same threshold. Image-level results are not proof of event-level recall.

Counting customers

          
Open a camera, assign an Entrance or Exit role, and tick Count as Customer before saving. Every crossing in the configured direction is then tagged as a customer event and appears here immediately. Confirmed customer entrances are counted as customers in the Overview; pending crossings are listed separately as Not confirmed. Removing the role also disables the customer flag.

Sign-in protection

          
Passwords are stored as salted PBKDF2 hashes and are never
            shown again. Sessions use an HttpOnly cookie and can be ended with
            Sign out. An account locks after three consecutive wrong
            passwords; use Unlock here to let that person try again.
            Disabling an account also ends its active sessions immediately.
